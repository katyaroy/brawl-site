About the game

Brawl is a fast top-down arena shooter where every hit pushes: shove foes into walls to splat them, ring them out, or hold off the Swarm together. Play 1 v 1 up to 6 v 6 or free-for-all against bots, work through the 100-level Career, or host a game on your phone for friends on the same Wi-Fi.

It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts, and everything except online play works offline.

Contacting support

For faults, crashes or feature requests, please write to swarog46@gmail.com.

To help us reproduce an issue, please include your device model, the operating system version, the version of the game, and what you were playing at the time (mode, map, online or against bots).

We endeavour to respond within thirty days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play with friends?

Everyone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi, or on one phone's personal hotspot. One player opens Play online and taps Host on this device. The others open Play online, pick that game from the list, and join a room or create one. Bots fill every empty seat.

My friend's game doesn't appear in the list

Check that both devices are on the same network. Guest and public Wi-Fi networks often stop devices from seeing each other; a personal hotspot works instead. On iPhone, allow Brawl in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. You can also type the address shown on the host's screen. The host must keep Brawl open: a game hosted on a phone stops when Brawl goes to the background.

What happens if my connection drops?

A bot keeps playing your fighter. Your seat is held for a minute: open Play online again and pick the same server, and you are back in the same match.

Can iPhone and Android players play together?

Yes. iPhone, iPad, Android and Mac players can join the same game.

How do the touch controls work?

Your left thumb anywhere on the left half of the screen moves. Your right thumb on the right half aims and shoots once it leaves the small ring in the middle; a quick tap there shoots the nearest foe. The buttons on the right dash, tether and fire your super. Stick size and aim speed can be changed under Controls on the main menu.

Does the game require an internet connection?

No. Every mode can be played offline against bots. A network is used only when you choose Play online.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Progress is not transferred between devices, and uninstalling the game removes it.

Does the game contain purchases or advertisements?

No. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, loot boxes or advertisements.
