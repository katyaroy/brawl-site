1. About this Policy

1.1 This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in Brawl: Wall Splat (the "Game"), an arena game for iOS and Android. It applies to all versions of the Game.

1.2 The Game is published by the independent developer of Brawl ("we", "us"), who is the data controller for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR").

1.3 This Policy does not cover the application stores that distribute the Game, your device's operating system, or a game server run by another person (clause 4.4).

2. Information we collect

2.1 We collect no personal data. The Game has no accounts or sign-in, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no tracking technologies and no third-party software development kits.

2.2 Playing alone or against bots uses no network connection at all. The Game connects to a network only when you choose Play online, as described in section 4.

2.3 We sell, share and disclose no information about you.

3. Information stored on your device

3.1 The Game stores on your device: your progress (career levels and stars, rank and unlocked items); your settings (sound, music, vibration and touch controls); the player name you use online; a random number created by the Game to tell your device apart in an online match; and the address of the last game server you joined.

3.2 This is written to the private storage area assigned to the Game by the operating system and is never sent to us. The random number is not derived from your device, your Apple or Google account, or any advertising identifier.

3.3 If you have enabled iCloud Backup or the Google backup service, Game data may be included in those backups, which are created and controlled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies and your device settings.

4. Online play

4.1 Online play is optional. When you open Play online, the Game looks for game servers on your local Wi-Fi network, or connects to a server address that you type.

4.2 While you play online, the Game sends to the server you chose: your player name; the random number described in clause 3.1; your chosen class, level and team; any room code and room password you enter; and your controls as you play. As with any internet connection, the server also receives your device's network address (IP address).

4.3 The server uses this information only to run the match. It keeps it in memory while the room is open, and for one minute after a disconnection so that you can rejoin the same match, and then discards it. The server software saves nothing to disk; a server run on a computer shows room events, with player names but not addresses, in its window while it runs. Other players in the same room see your player name, class and level.

4.4 A game server may be run by another player, on their phone or computer (Host on this device), or by us. A server run by another person is under that person's control, not ours.

4.5 The Game has no chat and no messaging. Your player name is the only text other players see; please do not use your real name or any other personal information as your player name. You can change it at any time on the Play online screen.

5. Permissions

5.1 Local network (iOS). iOS asks for this permission the first time you open Play online, so that the Game can find games hosted on your Wi-Fi. It may be declined or revoked in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network; every other part of the Game remains available.

5.2 Internet and vibration (Android). The Android edition uses the internet permission for online play only, and the vibration permission for haptic feedback, which may be switched off in the Game's settings.

6. Third parties

6.1 The App Store and Google Play may collect information in connection with your download of the Game. That collection is governed by Apple's and Google's privacy policies and is outside our control. See the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6.2 No other party receives any information from us, and we make no international data transfer.

7. Deleting your data

7.1 Everything the Game stores is on your device and under your control. Uninstalling the Game removes all of its data from the device.

7.2 Information held by a game server during a match is discarded when the room closes, as described in clause 4.3. We keep nothing that we could delete on your behalf.

8. Children's privacy

8.1 The Game contains no chat, messaging, user-generated content or social features beyond the player names described in clause 4.5.

8.2 We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the equivalent minimum age in the user's jurisdiction, because we collect no personal information from any user.

9. Your rights

9.1 Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you hold rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. As we keep no personal data relating to you, there is nothing to which such a request could relate. Your right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority is unaffected.

9.2 For residents of California: we have not sold or shared personal information in the preceding twelve months, and do not treat anyone differently for exercising a right under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA.

10. Changes and contact

10.1 Should this Policy change, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.

10.2 Questions about this Policy may be sent to swarog46@gmail.com. We aim to reply within thirty days.
